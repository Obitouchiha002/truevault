Cover mode

Hand someone your phone without a second thought

While the vault is locked, the vault tabs are not greyed out — they are gone. A disabled “Vault” tab tells whoever is holding the phone exactly what the app is, which is the one thing a cover is for.

Locked, it is a notes app

Two tabs: Notes and Settings. No unlock screen, no lock icon, no hint that anything else exists.

  • Auto-lock returns to Notes, never to a password prompt
  • Notes really work — write, pin, search, archive, 30-day trash
  • Notes live in a separate database that cannot reach the vault

Unlocked, everything appears

Press and hold the Notes title, enter your password, and the full app is there.

  • The gesture only opens the password screen — it never opens the vault
  • Settings → Open Private Vault does the same thing, visibly
  • Forgetting the gesture can never lock you out of your own files
What it does

Everything happens on your phone

There is no server to talk to. The app has no internet permission at all.

Encrypted vault

Photos, videos and documents are encrypted before they become vault items, with a separate key per file. On-disk names are random.

Secure Copy & Move

Copy keeps the original and says so. Move encrypts, verifies the copy opens, commits it, and only then asks Android to delete the original.

Leak scanner

Point it at a folder and it finds copies of files you already secured, by size and content hash — and states what it cannot see.

Send from anywhere

TrueVault appears in the Android share sheet for every file type. Share from your gallery, a chat or a download — it waits until you unlock.

Storage limit you set

Choose a ceiling from 500 MB to 20 GB, or none. It limits what comes in — nothing already stored is ever removed to make room.

Change the icon

Four launcher identities. The app tells you on that same screen that this does not hide it from Android Settings.

Encrypted backup

Export an archive and restore it into a brand-new vault with a different password. Every item still opens — file keys are re-wrapped on the way through.

Fingerprint or PIN

4-digit, 6-digit or a passphrase, with a growing delay after failed attempts. Android does the fingerprint check — the app never receives it.

Secure Move, step by step

No original is deleted before its encrypted copy is proven to open

This order never varies. A crash, a cancellation or a full disk at any point leaves your original exactly where it was.

Encrypt

The file streams through encryption into a temporary container. Nothing outside the app has been touched.

Verify

The container is read back and every authentication tag is checked. A copy that cannot be opened is never committed.

Commit

An atomic rename. From that instant the encrypted item either exists complete or does not exist at all.

Then ask

Only now does Android show its delete confirmation. Decline it and the app says the original remains — never that it was secured.

Privacy policy, in short

What the app touches, and what it keeps

Every line below comes from an audit of the app's own source. The full policy is bundled inside the app, so you can read it with no connection.

Files you select on device

Read only after you pick them through Android's own picker. Encrypted into the app's private storage. Never uploaded.

File names and sizes encrypted

Stored in a local database, sealed row by row. The container on disk is named with a random identifier, so the folder reveals nothing.

Your password never stored

Held in memory only while it is used, then the buffer is overwritten. What is stored is a sealed key blob, not the password.

Biometrics not received

Android performs the check and returns a yes or no. Your fingerprint or face never reaches the app.

Analytics none

No analytics SDK and no crash reporter. There is nothing to switch off, so the app does not show a consent screen for something that does not exist.

Network none

The app does not declare the internet permission and contains no network library. It has no way to send anything anywhere.

Installed apps launchable only

Private-app support reads launchable activities through Android's launcher API. It never asks to see every installed package, and stores nothing.

Deleting your call

Delete one item, delete all vault data, or reset the app entirely. Uninstalling removes everything the app stored.

What it will not claim

The honest part

Every privacy app promises the world. Here is what this one cannot do — stated for the same reason it is stated inside the app, on the screen where each choice is made.

It does not make the app invisible. Android Settings, storage and battery screens list it under its real name.
It cannot protect a rooted or already-compromised phone.
It cannot reach copies of your files it has no access to — other apps, chats, cloud accounts, another device.
A 4-digit PIN is weak. The app says so on the screen where you pick it.
Lose your password and your recovery key and the vault is gone. There is no account and no reset email.
No independent security audit has been performed on this app.

Why say any of this

Someone may rely on a feature like this in a situation that genuinely matters. Letting them believe it does more than it does would put them at risk on a promise the app cannot keep.

Uninstalling removes your vault. Everything is stored on your device and nowhere else. Export an encrypted backup and keep it somewhere safe.
Under the hood

Specifications

File encryptionAES-GCM, chunked, a separate key per file. Each chunk is bound to the file header and to its own position, so reordering or truncating a container is detected.
PasswordArgon2id, deliberately slow, with a per-vault salt.
Key storageSealed twice — once by your password, once by a key in the device's hardware-backed keystore. Useless without your password, and useless on another device.
4-digit PINAround 104 days on average to guess against the built-in delay. Measured, not estimated.
6-digit PINAround 28 years on the same basis.
PermissionsOne: USE_BIOMETRIC. No storage permission, no “all files access”, no internet.
Analytics & crash reportingNone.
Android backupDisabled, so your vault is never swept into a system backup you did not ask for.
RequiresAndroid 8.0 (API 26) or newer
