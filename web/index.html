Cover mode

Hand someone your phone without a second thought

While the vault is locked, the vault tabs are not greyed out — they are gone. A disabled “Vault” tab tells whoever is holding the phone exactly what the app is, which is the one thing a cover is for.

Locked, it is a notes app

Two tabs: Notes and Settings. No unlock screen, no lock icon, no hint that anything else exists.

  • Auto-lock returns to Notes, never to a password prompt
  • Notes really work — write, pin, search, archive, 30-day trash
  • Notes live in a separate database that cannot reach the vault

Unlocked, everything appears

Press and hold the Notes title, enter your password, and the full app is there — vault, scanner and all.

  • The gesture only opens the password screen. It never opens the vault.
  • Settings → Open Private Vault does the same thing, visibly
  • Forgetting the gesture can never lock you out of your own files
What it does

Everything happens on your phone

There is no server to talk to. The app has no internet permission at all.

Encrypted vault

Photos, videos and documents are encrypted before they become vault items, with a separate key per file. On-disk names are random, so the folder tells nobody what you kept.

Secure Copy & Secure Move

Copy keeps the original where it is and says so. Move encrypts, verifies the encrypted copy opens, commits it, and only then asks Android to delete the original.

Leak scanner

Point it at a folder and it finds copies of files you already secured, by size and content hash. It also states plainly what it cannot see.

Send from anywhere

TrueVault appears in the Android share sheet for every file type. Share from your gallery, a chat, a browser download — it waits, encrypted-side locked, until you unlock.

Storage limit you set

Choose a ceiling from 500 MB to 20 GB, or none. It limits what comes in — nothing already stored is ever removed or shrunk to make room.

Change the icon

Four launcher identities — TrueVault, Nexa, Nexa Notes, Nexa Files. The app tells you on that same screen that this does not hide it from Android Settings.

Encrypted backup

Export an encrypted archive and restore it into a brand-new vault with a different password. Every item still opens — the file keys are re-wrapped on the way through.

Fingerprint or PIN

4-digit, 6-digit or a passphrase, with a delay after failed attempts. Android does the fingerprint check — the app never receives your fingerprint.

Secure Move, step by step

No original is deleted before its encrypted copy is proven to open

This order never varies. A crash, a cancellation or a full disk at any point leaves your original exactly where it was.

Encrypt

The file streams through encryption into a temporary container. Nothing outside the app has been touched.

Verify

The container is read back and every authentication tag is checked. A copy that cannot be opened is never committed.

Commit

An atomic rename. From that instant the encrypted item either exists complete or does not exist at all.

Then ask

Only now does Android show its own delete confirmation. Decline it and the app says the original remains — never that it was secured.

What it will not claim

The honest part

Every privacy app promises the world. Here is what this one cannot do — stated here for the same reason it is stated inside the app, on the screen where each choice is made.

It does not make the app invisible. Android Settings, storage and battery screens list it under its real name.
It cannot protect a rooted or already-compromised phone.
It cannot reach copies of your files it has no access to — other apps, chats, cloud accounts, another device.
A 4-digit PIN is weak. The app says so on the screen where you pick it.
Lose your password and your recovery key and the vault is gone. There is no account and no reset email.
No independent security audit has been performed on this app.

Why say any of this

Someone may rely on a feature like this in a situation that genuinely matters. Letting them believe it does more than it does would put them at risk on a promise the app cannot keep.

Uninstalling removes your vault. Everything is stored on your device and nowhere else. Export an encrypted backup and keep it somewhere safe.
Under the hood

Specifications

File encryptionAES-GCM, chunked, a separate key per file. Each chunk is bound to the file header and to its own position, so reordering or truncating a container is detected rather than merely unlikely.
PasswordArgon2id, deliberately slow, with a per-vault salt.
Key storageSealed twice — once by your password, once by a key held in the device's hardware-backed keystore. Useless without your password, and useless on another device.
Guessing a 4-digit PINAround 104 days on average against the built-in delay. Measured, not estimated.
Guessing a 6-digit PINAround 28 years on the same basis.
Permissions requestedOne: USE_BIOMETRIC. No storage permission, no “all files access”, no internet.
Analytics & crash reportingNone. There is no SDK to switch off, so the app does not show you a consent screen for something that does not exist.
Android backupDisabled, so your vault is never swept into a system backup you did not ask for.
